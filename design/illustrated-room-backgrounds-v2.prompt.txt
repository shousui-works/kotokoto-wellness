## Portrait

Use case: style-transfer.

Primary request: redraw this same room as a distinctly illustrated, gently stylized 2D cozy game environment. The current image is too photographic. Make the transformation unmistakable: hand-painted animation background, rounded slightly simplified furniture forms, clean soft warm-brown contour lines on key edges, broad smooth pastel color areas with subtle brush texture, softly painted cel-like shadows. Not flat vector art, not a photo with a filter, not realistic 3D rendering.
Keep the current portrait composition, room architecture, furniture positions and camera framing: sage green sofa to the back left, tall cream-curtained window toward center-left, rounded honey-oak sideboard at the back right, simple botanical wall picture and restrained leafy plant. Preserve the full-screen pulled-back view and spacious walkable floor in the lower 60%. Remove realistic wood pores, fabric fibers, individual detailed leaves, lens effects, photographic reflections and physically exact sunlight patterns.
Palette: warm ivory, pale honey oak, muted sage, creamy beige. Cozy diffused afternoon sunshine rendered as a few broad soft golden patches and simplified gentle window shadows. Keep contrast gentle and the floor readable for a cream dog. Make the scene more charming through simplified proportions and illustrated shapes, not through extra objects.
Invariants: same room, same furnishings, same wide open floor, same portrait orientation, opaque full-bleed background. Top and bottom areas stay calm for overlaid game controls. NO dog, animal, person, pet bed, face on furniture, text, label, watermark, logo, border, UI, button or icon.
