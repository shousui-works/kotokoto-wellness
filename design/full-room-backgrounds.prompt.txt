# Full-screen room backgrounds

Created with built-in image generation. Portrait and landscape backgrounds depict the same warm room; the dog is rendered separately.

## Portrait

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: a full-screen portrait BACKGROUND for a cozy Japanese mobile pet dog game called こつこつ日和. NO CHARACTERS OR UI.
Create a beautiful warm lived-in Japanese / Scandinavian living room, seen from a pulled-back wide camera at low human sitting height, looking into the room. Portrait 9:16 composition, full bleed to all four edges. Softly realistic high-quality 3D game environment rendering with warm natural daylight, soft tactile materials and gentle depth. Muted cream, warm honey oak, pale sage and linen. The room should feel spacious and peaceful, not a close-up vignette and not an isometric dollhouse.
LAYOUT FOR MOBILE GAMEPLAY: furniture occupies the UPPER 40% of the canvas and outermost side edges. The LOWER 60% is mostly open, uninterrupted warm pale oak flooring where an animated dog will roam. NO central coffee table, NO foreground furniture obstructing the floor. Establish the floor-to-back-wall line around 38% of canvas height, leaving a generous uninterrupted play area. A broad, very subtle, pale warm ivory woven oval rug lies around 65% canvas height, occupying about 65% canvas width; its near edge ends around 78% height, leaving a bare foreground floor. Clear floor remains on both sides of the rug. Keep important furniture inside the central 85% width so gentle mobile cropping still works.
BACK ROOM: a soft small sage-green linen sofa tucked against the left back wall; a tall window toward center-left with airy translucent cream curtains and a glimpse of soft leafy greenery outside; a low rounded natural-oak sideboard against the right back wall, with a quiet ceramic vase and two or three books. One restrained houseplant near the outer right corner. Back wall warm off-white plaster with a tiny framed botanical print. These are fixed furnishings, modest scale to show a complete room from a distance.
LIGHTING: afternoon sunshine enters from upper left through the curtains, producing soft beautiful diffuse pools of sunlight and subtle long diagonal window shadows on the wood floor, while keeping the open floor evenly readable for a cream-colored dog. Soft shadows, no dramatic high contrast, no overexposure, no lens flare.
COMPOSITION: sense of a complete room and lots of breathing space; clean real interior perspective. Camera framed wide enough that the whole back sofa/window/sideboard area is comfortably visible and the floor feels large. No panoramic distortion. Top 12% of the wall and bottom 14% of the floor stay visually calm because subtle game controls will later overlay them.
NO dog, cat, animal, person, pet bed, text, logo, caption, watermark, border, UI, buttons, icons or speech bubble. No visually busy patterns, no excessive decoration, no bright saturated colors. This is a premium room background, not a screenshot of an interface.

## Landscape

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: wide landscape background for the same full-screen pet companion room.
Use the attached portrait room as the edit target / exact room reference. Make a wide landscape 16:9 version of THIS SAME warm Japanese-Scandinavian living room, preserving the pale sage linen sofa, cream sheer curtains, tall sunlit window with leafy greenery, low rounded honey-oak sideboard, tiny botanical print, ceramic vase/books, restrained corner houseplant, pale oval woven rug, soft ivory plaster walls, warm natural oak floor and soft afternoon sun. Same premium softly realistic 3D cozy-game rendering and lighting.
Pull the camera BACK to show a wider complete room with a broad open floor. Furniture stays against the back wall in the upper 42% of the image and near the extreme outer edges; the lower 58% is an unobstructed open warm oak floor for a small animated dog to roam. The pale oval rug is centered in the mid-floor around 65% of image height and ends around 79% height, with clear bare floor in the foreground. Keep furniture naturally proportioned, never stretch the portrait room. Expand the room architecture horizontally and reveal more wall/floor on both sides with believable interior perspective.
No central coffee table or large foreground object; keep the middle floor empty. Gentle diagonal window-light patches across the floor, soft shadows, no extreme contrast, no overexposure. Top 12% wall and bottom 14% floor remain calm for game controls. Full bleed, no border. NO DOG, pet, animal, person, pet bed, text, label, logo, watermark, icons, UI, buttons or speech bubble.
